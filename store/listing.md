**Promotional text (170):**


**Description (4000):**

```
A Method for Prayer helps you pray in the words of Scripture, with the book by Matthew Henry.

Henry first published A Method for Prayer in 1710. He divides prayer into six parts: adoration, confession, petition, thanksgiving, intercession and the conclusion. Under each head, he gives the words of the Bible that are proper to it.

PRAY NOW
• A guided prayer through the six parts, in order
• Each day shows the next heads of each part
• You pray through each part in 30 days or fewer
• Tap "More" to see the whole head
• Tap a reference to read the verse in the King James Version, with the verses around it

THE BOOK
• Henry's preface and the nine chapters
• Each head, with its paragraphs of prayer and its references
• A paraphrase of the Lord's Prayer in the words of Scripture

OCCASIONS
• Morning and evening prayer, the Lord's Day, and meals
• The Lord's Supper, baptism, marriage, sickness and other occasions
• Henry's forms of prayer for families and for children

INTERCESSION LIST
• Add the names and the needs that you pray for
• The guided prayer shows them in the part for intercession
• The list stays on your device

THE OTHER APPS
• Memorise a verse in Hearers
• Read the notes of John Brown in Haddington Bible

PRIVATE
A Method for Prayer has no account, no advertisements and no tracking. Your settings and your list stay on your device. All the text is in the app, so it works with no connection.

FREE
A Method for Prayer is free. Henry's text and the King James Version are in the public domain. The text comes from a 19th-century printing.
```

**What's new (version 1):**

```
The first release.
```

**App Privacy:** select "Data Not Collected". The app has no analytics, no account and no network
requests of its own.

**Notes for the reviewer:**

```
No account is needed. Tap "Pray now" to go through the six parts. Tap a green reference to read the verse in a sheet. The intercession list stays on the device. Links to other apps open those apps, or the in-app browser.
```
